Everything the patch adds to stock 2.3.4, on two pages to build your controller mappings from.

Each input as Gate, CV or AudioTOOLS twice: the Input screen

  • Live Flip modes and Hold FX need the Clip pad's Clip mode on Vinyl, Jungle or Key Lock.
  • CV Action reads one step per semitone from 0 V: C is Off, C# the first choice, and so on.
  • Keep CCs carries what your controller sent into the next preset. Screen edits stay with their preset.
